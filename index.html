No lap lanes in the warm pool on weekends. Friday morning 8:30–10a is Happy Splashers (parent & baby) in the zero-depth area; Saturday 8–9a is Aqua Conditioning on the lap side. Camps run weekday late mornings on the lap side.

Lap swimmers

  • Mornings = 8 lanes in the Competition Pool until 1p, every day.
  • Afternoons = 4 lanes (6 on Sunday afternoons), alongside rec swim.
  • Warm-water laps: 3 lanes in the Activity Pool, Mon–Fri 6–8:45a only.
  • Fri & Sat run until 8pm — easy evening swims.

Families & open swim

  • Warm pool, lesson-free: Mon–Thu 6a–1p & 2–4p · Fri 10a–1p & 2–8p · Sat 12:30–7p · Sun 12:30–6p.
  • Tubes + diving boards daily during rec swim: Mon–Thu 2–5p · Fri 2–8p · Sat 1–8p · Sun 1–6p (swim test required).
  • Cool-pool open swim mornings too: 4 lanes until 1p every day.
  • Zero-depth beach entry on the warm side for the littlest swimmers.

Lessons, classes & camps

  • Swim lessons: Mon–Thu 4–7p + Sat/Sun mornings until 12:30p, in the Activity Pool.
  • Camps: Mon–Fri 9a–1p on the warm pool's lap side.
  • Happy Splashers (parent & baby): Fri 8:30–10a · Aqua Conditioning: Sat 8–9a.
  • Register at rec.us/piedmont.

Beat the crowds

  • Early afternoon is peak — the Activity Pool has hit capacity on busy days.
  • Passholders get priority over drop-ins when near capacity.
  • Call ahead: (510) 420-3090 to check capacity.
  • Quietest bets: weekday mornings and the last 2 hours of the day.
