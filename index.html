Zero-depth and lap sides open on separate windows most of the week (see per-day blocks); "Both" periods mean the whole Activity Pool is open. No reserved-swim block this week — the city's table shows plain open swim all weekend (Sat 7a–8p, Sun 7a–6p) with no swim lessons in the Classes row, so rec.us/piedmont has no reservation needed for either day. Weekday classes: HIIT Mon 12–1p, Aqua Aerobics Tue/Thu 10–11a, Happy Splashers Fri 10:30–11:30a (no class Wednesday) — all run alongside open swim, which continues at its full published span.

Lap swimmers

  • Lane counts jump around by day: Wed starts at 6 lanes 6–8a before jumping to 10; Mon/Tue/Thu/Fri run a straight 10-lane 6a–1p block.
  • Thursday afternoon is a wash: the Competition Pool closes entirely 2–8p Sept 3 for a PHS water polo game — no lap swim after 1p that day.
  • Evenings vary with team practice: Mon polo takes over 5–7p, Wed/Fri polo runs 6–8p (full pool), with swim team sharing 4–6p Tue/Wed/Fri.
  • Weekend mornings jump to 10 lanes: 7–9a Sat, 7a–12p Sun, before dropping to 5 lanes the rest of the day.
  • Warm-water laps: 3 lanes in the Activity Pool, Mon–Fri 6–9:30a only.

Families & open swim

  • Warm pool open swim: roughly 6a–1p and 2–7p on weekdays (Fri to 8p), 7a–8p Sat, 7a–6p Sun — no reservation needed anywhere this week.
  • Tubes + diving boards during rec swim: Mon 2–5p · Tue/Wed/Fri 2–4p · Sat 11a–8p · Sun 12–6p (swim test required). None Thursday — the Competition Pool is closed that afternoon for the water polo game.
  • Zero-depth beach entry on the warm side for the littlest swimmers, open every morning from 6a (7a weekends).

Lessons & classes

  • No swim lessons this week — the Activity Pool's Classes row is empty Sat/Sun, so no reservation is required for weekend open swim.
  • HIIT: Mon 12–1p · Aqua Aerobics: Tue & Thu 10–11a · Happy Splashers (parent & baby): Fri 10:30–11:30a (shorter than the usual 10:30a–12p) — no class Wednesday.
  • Swim team practice continues Tue/Wed/Fri 4–6p in the Competition Pool (5 lanes) — separate from the Activity Pool classes above.
  • Register for classes at rec.us/piedmont.

Beat the crowds

  • Thursday, Sept 3, 2–8p: Competition Pool closed for a PHS water polo game — spectators enter at the Magnolia gate; Activity Pool stays open as usual.
  • Weekday evenings are tighter than usual — polo and swim team practice cut into Competition Pool lap access after ~4–5p most weekdays.
  • Passholders get priority over drop-ins when near capacity.
  • Call ahead: (510) 420-3090 to check capacity.
