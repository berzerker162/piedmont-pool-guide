Lap swimmers

  • Monday is Labor Day: holiday hours 7a–6p, straight 10-lane block 7a–1p then 5 lanes 1–6p, no team practice cutting into it.
  • Tue/Thu mornings dip to 6 lanes 6–7:30a for a short polo block before ramping to 10 lanes; Wed/Fri/Sat/Sun run their usual patterns.
  • Wednesday afternoon is a wash: the Competition Pool closes entirely 2–8p Sept 9 for a PHS water polo game — no lap swim after 1p that day.
  • Evenings vary with team practice: Tue/Thu/Fri swim team shares 4–6p, and polo runs evenings Tue 6–7:30p, Thu 6–9p(!), Fri 6–8p.
  • Weekend mornings jump to 10 lanes: 7–9a Sat, 7a–12p Sun, before dropping to 5 lanes the rest of the day.
  • Warm-water laps: 3 lanes in the Activity Pool, weekdays only (7–9:30a Mon, 6–9:30a Wed–Fri; none Tuesday).

Families & open swim

  • Warm pool open swim: roughly 6a–1p and 2–7p on weekdays (Mon holiday hours 7a–6p straight through; Fri to 8p), 7a–8p Sat, 7a–6p Sun.
  • Weekend mornings need a reservation: Activity Pool 9a–12p Sat/Sun is reserved swim during lessons — book at rec.us before you go.
  • Tubes + diving boards during rec swim: Mon 1–6p (holiday) · Tue/Thu/Fri 2–4p · Sat 11a–8p · Sun 12–6p (swim test required). None Wednesday — Competition Pool closed that afternoon for the water polo game.
  • Zero-depth beach entry on the warm side for the littlest swimmers, open every morning (from 6a weekdays, 7a Mon/weekends).

Lessons & classes

  • Weekend swim lessons return: Sat/Sun 8a–12p in the Activity Pool, with reserved open swim 9a–12p alongside them.
  • No Monday HIIT this week (Labor Day holiday schedule) · Aqua Aerobics: Tue & Thu 10–11a · Happy Splashers (parent & baby): Fri 10:30–11:30a — no class Wednesday.
  • Swim team practice shifts to Tue/Thu/Fri 4–6p in the Competition Pool (5 lanes) — separate from the Activity Pool classes above.
  • Register for classes at rec.us/piedmont — note the weekend reserved-swim section wasn't posted there yet as of this update.

Beat the crowds

  • Wednesday, Sept 9, 2–8p: Competition Pool closed for a PHS water polo game — spectators enter at the Magnolia gate; Activity Pool stays open as usual.
  • Thursday evening runs long: polo practice continues to 9p, later than the pool's normal 7p weekday close for the public.
  • Passholders get priority over drop-ins when near capacity.
  • Call ahead: (510) 420-3090 to check capacity.
