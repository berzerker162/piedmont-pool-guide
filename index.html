Zero-depth and lap sides open on separate windows most of the week (see per-day blocks); "Both" periods mean the whole Activity Pool is open. Purple = reserved open swim (free with entry, 50 spots) at rec.us/piedmont — weekend mornings only (8a–12p Sat–Sun); the bookable slot posted on rec.us is sometimes narrower than the city's listed window, so check before you go. Mon–Thu 4–7p needs no reservation — open swim runs 2–7p on both sides alongside swim lessons. Weekday HIIT/Aqua Aerobics classes and Fri Happy Splashers (10:30a–12p) also run alongside open swim; see the Lessons card for details.

Lap swimmers

  • Lane counts now vary by day: Wed lap doesn't start until 8:30a; Tue/Thu open with a 6-lane 6–7:30a slot before jumping to 8 lanes at 7:30a; Mon/Fri run a straight 8-lane 6a–1p block.
  • Evenings end earlier this week as team practice takes over: lap swim wraps by 5:30p most weekdays, 4:30p on Monday.
  • Weekend mornings jump to 10 lanes (was 8): 7–8:30a Sat, 7a–12p Sun, before dropping to 4 lanes the rest of the day.
  • Warm-water laps: 3 lanes in the Activity Pool, Mon–Fri 6–9:30a only.
  • Sat still runs until 8pm for an easy evening swim.

Families & open swim

  • Warm pool open swim: 6a–1p and 2–7p on weekdays (split by zero-depth vs. lap side varies by day), 12–8p Sat, 12–6p Sun.
  • Tubes + diving boards during rec swim, this week: Mon 2–4:30p · Tue/Wed/Thu/Fri 2–5:30p · Sat 11a–8p · Sun 12–6p (swim test required).
  • Cool-pool open swim windows shift later some days: Wed doesn't open until 8:30a, Tue/Thu until 8a (mornings otherwise 6a–1p Mon/Fri).
  • Zero-depth beach entry on the warm side for the littlest swimmers.

Lessons & classes

  • Swim lessons: Mon–Thu 4–7p + Sat/Sun mornings 8a–12p, in the Activity Pool.
  • HIIT: Mon 12–1p · Aqua Aerobics: Tue & Thu 10–11a · Happy Splashers (parent & baby): Fri 10:30a–12p.
  • New this week: swim team practice Tue–Fri 4–6p in the Competition Pool (5 lanes) — separate from Activity Pool lessons.
  • Reservations are only needed for weekend-morning Activity Pool open swim (Sat–Sun 8a–12p, 50 spots/session) — book at rec.us/piedmont. Weekday 4–7p lesson hours do not require one, and Competition Pool open/lap swim never does.

Beat the crowds

  • Early afternoon is peak — the Activity Pool has hit capacity on busy days.
  • Weekday evenings are tighter than usual — polo and swim team practice cut into Competition Pool lap access after 4:30–5:30p Mon–Fri.
  • Passholders get priority over drop-ins when near capacity, and get first crack at reserved-swim signups (Thu 9a vs. Fri/Sat for drop-ins).
  • Call ahead: (510) 420-3090 to check capacity.
