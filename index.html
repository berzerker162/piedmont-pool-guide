Zero-depth and lap sides now open on separate windows most of the week (see per-day blocks); "Both" periods mean the whole Activity Pool is open. Purple = reserved open swim (free with entry, 50 spots) at rec.us/piedmont — now 4–7p Mon–Thu and 8a–12p Sat–Sun. Weekday HIIT/Aqua Aerobics classes and Fri Happy Splashers (10:30a–12p) run alongside open swim; see the Lessons card for details.

Lap swimmers

  • Mornings = 8 lanes in the Competition Pool: until 12p Mon–Thu, until 1p Fri/Sat/Sun.
  • Afternoons = 4 lanes most days; Sat afternoon jumps to 4 lanes from 1p, evenings stay 4 lanes.
  • Water polo preseason takes the full Competition Pool Mon–Thu 12–2p — lap swim pauses that window.
  • Warm-water laps: 3 lanes in the Activity Pool, Mon–Fri 6–9:30a only (weekend warm lap slot from June has ended).
  • Fri & Sat run until 8pm — easy evening swims.

Families & open swim

  • Warm pool open swim: generally 6a–1p and 2–4p on weekdays (exact split varies by day), 2–7p Fri, 12–8p Sat, 12–6p Sun.
  • Tubes + diving boards daily during rec swim, this week: Mon–Thu 2–5p · Fri 2–8p · Sat 1–8p · Sun 1–6p (swim test required).
  • Cool-pool open swim mornings too, this week: 4 lanes Mon–Thu until 12p, plus 5–7p evenings Mon–Thu.
  • Zero-depth beach entry on the warm side for the littlest swimmers.

Lessons & classes

  • Swim lessons: Mon–Thu 4–7p + Sat/Sun mornings 8a–12p, in the Activity Pool.
  • HIIT: Mon 12–1p · Aqua Aerobics: Tue & Thu 10–11a · Happy Splashers (parent & baby): Fri 10:30a–12p.
  • Weekday Camps and Saturday Aqua Conditioning are no longer on the city's published schedule.
  • Activity Pool open swim during lesson hours requires a reservation (50 spots/session): Mon–Thu 4–7p · Sat–Sun 8a–12p. Book at rec.us/piedmont.

Beat the crowds

  • Early afternoon is peak — the Activity Pool has hit capacity on busy days.
  • Passholders get priority over drop-ins when near capacity, and get first crack at reserved-swim signups (Thu 9a vs. Fri/Sat for drop-ins).
  • Call ahead: (510) 420-3090 to check capacity.
  • Quietest bets: weekday mornings and the last 2 hours of the day.
